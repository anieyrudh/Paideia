# Transfer Problem: Electric-Field Analogy

You now meet an electric field around a positive point charge. Without using any new formula beyond the analogy, decide what should correspond to source mass, test mass, field strength, force, and potential. Then state one sign or direction difference that must be handled with care.

## Expected reasoning

- Source mass `M` corresponds to source charge `Q`: it creates the field.
- Test mass `m` corresponds to test charge `q`: it experiences a force because it is in the field.
- Gravitational field strength `g = F / m` corresponds to electric field strength `E = F / q`.
- Gravitational force is always attractive, while electric force can be attractive or repulsive depending on charge signs.
- Gravitational potential around an attractive source is negative when zero is at infinity; electric potential sign depends on the sign of the source charge.

## Rubric

- Separates source property from test object: 1 mark.
- Maps field strength as force per unit test object property: 1 mark.
- Explains why force changes with test object while field at a point does not: 1 mark.
- Identifies at least one sign or direction difference: 1 mark.
- Uses the analogy without claiming gravity and electric fields are identical: 1 mark.
