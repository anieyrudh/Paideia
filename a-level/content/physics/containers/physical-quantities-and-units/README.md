# Physical Quantities and Units

This is the first learner-facing A-Level Physics product slice for quantity,
value, and unit discipline. It establishes the habit of asking what physical
quantity is being measured before manipulating numbers.

## What this teaches

Students learn to distinguish:

- a physical quantity from its numerical value and unit,
- base quantities from derived quantities,
- scalar records from vector records where direction matters,
- unit-consistent equations from impossible equations.

## What the student does

- Predicts whether an acceleration record is base, derived, unitless, or scalar.
- Sorts a card wall of measurement records into base, derived, unit-only, and number-only categories.
- Marks whether direction belongs to each record.
- Checks formula/unit reasoning such as `speed = distance ÷ time` and `force = mass × acceleration`.
- Uses an impossible-equation detector to reject equations whose left and right units do not match.
