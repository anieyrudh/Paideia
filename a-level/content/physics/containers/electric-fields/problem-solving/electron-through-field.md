# Transfer Problem: Electron Through a Field

## Prompt

An electron moves through a 240 V potential difference between two plates. Calculate the change in electric potential energy and explain why the electron accelerates in the direction opposite to the electric field.

## Worked Structure

1. Identify the moving charge: an electron has `q = -1.60 x 10^-19 C`.
2. Use the energy relationship:

```text
Delta U = q Delta V
```

3. Substitute:

```text
Delta U = (-1.60 x 10^-19 C)(240 V)
Delta U = -3.84 x 10^-17 J
```

4. Interpret the sign: the electron's electric potential energy decreases by `3.84 x 10^-17 J` for this move, so the field does positive work on it.
5. Direction check: the electric field direction is the force direction on a positive charge, so an electron experiences force opposite to the electric field.

## Rubric

- Correctly uses the electron charge with a negative sign.
- Computes the energy change in joules.
- Distinguishes field direction from force direction on a negative charge.
- States the physical meaning of the negative energy change.
