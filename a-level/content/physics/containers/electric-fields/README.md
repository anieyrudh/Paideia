## What this teaches

Predict field direction, calculate force on a charge, and connect electric potential to energy transfer.

An electric field describes how a source charge changes the space around it. At any point, the electric field strength is the force per unit positive test charge placed at that point. That definition separates the field made by the source from the force felt by a particular test charge.
