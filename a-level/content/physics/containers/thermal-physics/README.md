## What this teaches

Connect temperature, kelvin conversion, ideal-gas pressure, and thermal energy transfer.

Temperature is a state variable that tells the direction of net energy transfer by heating. Two bodies at the same temperature are in thermal equilibrium: no net thermal energy flows between them. Thermal energy transfer is not the same quantity as temperature; the energy needed to change a body's temperature also depends on its mass and material.
