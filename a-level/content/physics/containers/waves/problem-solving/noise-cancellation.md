# Noise-Cancellation Transfer Problem

Noise-cancelling headphones reduce the sound reaching the ear by playing a
second sound wave with similar amplitude and nearly opposite phase to the
incoming noise.

Use:

```latex
y_{\text{resultant}} = y_1 + y_2
```

Explain why the resultant displacement can become small even though the
individual waves still exist, and identify which quantities should remain
unchanged when the cancellation is ideal: wavelength, period, and frequency.
