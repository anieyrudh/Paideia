# Momentum and Impulse Problem-Solving Algorithm

1. Draw the displacement direction.
2. Resolve or identify the force component along the displacement.
3. Calculate work: `W = F s cos(theta)`.
4. Decide whether the work transfers energy into or out of the kinetic store.
5. If a time is given, calculate average power: `P = W / t`.
6. Check the sign, unit, and physical story.

For the lift-motor comparison, the work is the same when the load and height
are the same. The faster motor has the larger average power because it transfers
the same energy in less time.
