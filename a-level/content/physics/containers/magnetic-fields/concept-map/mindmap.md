# Magnetic Fields Mindmap

- Magnetic fields
  - Motor effect
    - Force on a current-carrying conductor
    - \(F = BIL\sin\theta\)
    - Direction from Fleming's left-hand rule
  - Moving charges
    - \(F = |q|vB\sin\theta\)
    - Positive charges follow the conventional-current rule
    - Negative charges reverse the force direction
    - Stationary charges have no magnetic force
  - Circular paths
    - Magnetic force can supply centripetal force
    - \(Bqv = mv^2/r\) for perpendicular motion
    - \(r = mv/(|q|B)\)
  - Direction traps
    - Force is not along the field
    - Parallel motion gives zero force
    - Field into page and out of page reverse the left-hand result
