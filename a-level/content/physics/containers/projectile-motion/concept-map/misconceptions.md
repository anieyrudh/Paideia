# Projectile Motion Misconceptions

## Horizontal And Vertical Motion Affect Each Other Directly

The prediction asks whether horizontal velocity changes while a horizontal
launch falls. The revealed trajectory shows gravity bending the vertical
position while the horizontal component remains constant in the no-drag model.

## A Continuing Forward Force Is Needed

Learners often explain forward motion with an imagined force that keeps
pushing the projectile. The model instead uses inertia horizontally and
downward gravitational acceleration vertically.

## Launch Angle Changes Gravity

Changing the angle changes `u_x` and `u_y`; it does not change `g`. The formula
panel keeps `g` fixed while the trajectory changes through the velocity
components.
