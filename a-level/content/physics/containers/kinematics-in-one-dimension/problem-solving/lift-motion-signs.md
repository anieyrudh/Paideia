# Transfer Problem: Lift Motion Signs

A lift is moving upward at `1.5 m s^-1` when it begins a constant acceleration
of `-0.50 m s^-2` for `2.0 s`.

## Worked route

Choose upward as positive.

Known values:

```text
u = +1.5 m s^-1
a = -0.50 m s^-2
t = 2.0 s
```

Displacement:

```text
s = ut + 1/2 at^2
s = (1.5)(2.0) + 1/2(-0.50)(2.0)^2
s = 3.0 - 1.0
s = +2.0 m
```

Final velocity:

```text
v = u + at
v = 1.5 + (-0.50)(2.0)
v = +0.50 m s^-1
```

The lift is still moving upward, but it is slowing down because acceleration
points opposite to its velocity.

## Rubric

- Chooses and states upward as the positive direction.
- Substitutes `u`, `a`, and `t` with correct signs and units.
- Calculates displacement as `+2.0 m`.
- Calculates final velocity as `+0.50 m s^-1`.
- Explains that negative acceleration means acceleration is downward, not that the lift has already moved downward.
