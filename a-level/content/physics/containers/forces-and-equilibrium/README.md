# Forces and Equilibrium

Balance perpendicular forces by making the resultant force zero in both horizontal and vertical directions.

## What Students Do

1. Predict the support forces needed to balance a fixed left pull and weight.
2. Adjust the rightward and upward support forces.
3. Reveal the net force and the formula used: `sum Fx = 0`, `sum Fy = 0`.
4. Transfer the same rule to a hanging sign problem.
