# Transfer Problem: Car Suspension After A Bump

A car body oscillates after a bump. Compare three changes:

1. the bump gives a larger amplitude,
2. passengers make the car heavier,
3. the suspension spring is made stiffer.

Explain which changes alter the period in the ideal spring model, and which
change only alters the size of the motion and the energy stored.

## Rubric

- Identifies the ideal model: `T = 2pi sqrt(m / k)`.
- States that larger amplitude does not change the ideal period.
- States that larger mass increases period.
- States that larger spring stiffness decreases period.
- Connects larger amplitude to larger stored energy.
- Avoids claiming acceleration is greatest at equilibrium.
