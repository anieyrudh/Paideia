# Oscillation Problem-Solving Algorithm

1. **Name the equilibrium position.** Decide where `x = 0` and which direction is positive.
2. **Check the restoring rule.** For simple harmonic motion, acceleration must have the form `a = -omega^2 x`.
3. **Convert timing quantities.** Use `omega = 2pi f`, `T = 1/f`, and `T = 2pi / omega`.
4. **Use the displacement model.** Apply `x(t) = A cos(omega t + phi)` or the equivalent sine form given in the question.
5. **Read velocity and acceleration signs.** Velocity shows direction of motion; acceleration points towards equilibrium.
6. **Interpret energy.** At turning points, potential energy is maximum and kinetic energy is zero. At equilibrium, kinetic energy is maximum and acceleration is zero.

The most common error is to import intuition from everyday large-amplitude
oscillations into the ideal SHM model. In the ideal spring model, amplitude
changes energy, not the period.
