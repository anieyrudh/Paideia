# Misconception Map

## Amplitude changes the period for every oscillator

**Evidence:** OpenStax College Physics 2e, Section 16.3, gives the ideal mass-spring period from mass and spring constant. Amplitude is not in the period expression for this ideal model.

**Surface in predict?** Yes. The prediction asks what happens to period when amplitude is doubled while mass and spring stiffness stay fixed.

**Correction:** Larger amplitude means a larger maximum displacement and larger stored energy. It does not change the ideal period unless the oscillator leaves the ideal SHM assumptions.

## Acceleration is greatest at equilibrium

**Evidence:** OpenStax University Physics Volume 1, Section 15.1, identifies acceleration in simple harmonic motion as proportional to displacement and directed towards equilibrium.

**Surface in predict?** No. It is surfaced in the observation and explanation stages by comparing displacement, velocity, and acceleration readouts.

**Correction:** Speed is greatest at equilibrium, but acceleration is zero there because displacement from equilibrium is zero.

## Zero displacement means zero speed and zero energy

**Evidence:** OpenStax University Physics Volume 1, Section 15.2, treats SHM energy as exchange between kinetic and potential energy stores.

**Surface in predict?** No. It is surfaced by the energy bars and transfer explanation.

**Correction:** At equilibrium, elastic potential energy is minimum and kinetic energy is maximum in the ideal model. The total energy is not zero.
