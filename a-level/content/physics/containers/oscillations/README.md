## What this teaches

Connect restoring acceleration, period, frequency, and energy exchange in simple harmonic motion.

An oscillation is a repeated motion about an equilibrium position. In simple harmonic motion, the acceleration is proportional to the displacement from equilibrium and points back towards equilibrium.
