# Misconceptions

## Magnitude-only vector addition

**Evidence**: OpenStax University Physics Volume 1, Section 2.1; Van Deventer and Wittmann (2015), _Physical Review Special Topics - Physics Education Research_.

**Surface in predict?** yes

**Description**: Students may add vector lengths as ordinary scalar quantities, predicting that two 5 m perpendicular displacements must produce 10 m.

## Negative scalar does not reverse direction

**Evidence**: Barniol (2011), _Physics Education Research Conference Proceedings_.

**Surface in predict?** no

**Description**: Students may understand scalar multiplication as stretching or shrinking a vector, but miss that multiplication by a negative scalar reverses direction.
