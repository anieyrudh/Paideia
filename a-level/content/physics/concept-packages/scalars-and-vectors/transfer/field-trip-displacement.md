# Transfer Problem · Field Trip Displacement

A class walks 300 m east from the school gate to a bus stop, then 400 m north
to a field site. A student says the class is 700 m from the school gate because
the two distances can be added.

1. State whether the 300 m and 400 m quantities should be combined as scalars
   or as vectors when finding displacement from the school gate.
2. Calculate the displacement magnitude from the school gate to the field site.
3. Explain why the total walking distance and the displacement magnitude are
   not the same physical quantity.

## Rubric

- Identifies displacement as a vector quantity because direction is part of the
  physical meaning.
- Uses perpendicular components to obtain a 500 m displacement magnitude.
- Distinguishes path length travelled from straight-line displacement.
