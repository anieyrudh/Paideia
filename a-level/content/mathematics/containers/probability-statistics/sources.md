## Claims Supported

- Probability distributions assign probabilities to random-variable values.
- Expected value is a long-run weighted average, not necessarily the most likely outcome.
- Variance and standard deviation measure spread around the expected value.
- A one-sample z-style decision compares an observed sample mean with a null mean in standard-error units.
