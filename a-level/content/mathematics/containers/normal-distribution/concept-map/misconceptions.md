# Misconception Audit

## z-score is a probability

The prediction prompt surfaces this directly by offering "The z-score itself is the probability area." The observe stage counters it by showing z as a standardised location and the probability as an area label.

## Symmetry makes every interval equally likely

The central-band versus upper-tail prediction forces learners to compare equal-distance and non-mirrored regions. The explanation stage states that symmetry only applies to mirrored regions on the same z-scale.

## Standard deviation has no unit

The formula legend names marks for \(X\), \(\mu\), and \(\sigma\), then shows the unit cancellation that produces a unitless z-score.
