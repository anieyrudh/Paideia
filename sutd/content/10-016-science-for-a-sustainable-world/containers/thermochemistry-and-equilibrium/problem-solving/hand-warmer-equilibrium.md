# Hand Warmer Equilibrium

A reusable hand warmer that gets warmer is transferring heat to its surroundings. Temperature evidence tells us the direction and size of energy transfer.

Equilibrium composition needs concentration or phase data. The heat sign alone does not prove that products dominate the mixture. A concentration quotient or equilibrium constant comparison is needed to make that claim.

The same concept transfers: heat flow and equilibrium composition are related, but they are not the same measurement.
