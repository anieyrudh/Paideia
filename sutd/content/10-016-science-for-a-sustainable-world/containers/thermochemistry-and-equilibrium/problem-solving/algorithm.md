# Algorithm

1. Identify the measured temperature change and convert it to kelvin difference.
2. Compute heat transferred to the sample with `q = mc Delta T`.
3. Interpret the sign: positive means the sample absorbed heat; negative means it released heat.
4. Identify current reactant and product concentrations.
5. Compute the reaction quotient from the concentration ratio.
6. Interpret the quotient as composition evidence, not as a heat-flow measurement.
7. Combine both evidence streams carefully when explaining a real reaction system.
