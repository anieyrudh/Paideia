# Problem-solving algorithm

1. Define the service requirement: load, stiffness, reuse count, and local recovery pathway.
2. Choose a material index that matches the requirement, such as strength per density or strength per density-carbon.
3. Rank candidate polymers with cited property records.
4. Estimate residual waste from the collection rate and reuse cycles.
5. Reject conclusions that rely only on a recycling symbol or biodegradable label.
