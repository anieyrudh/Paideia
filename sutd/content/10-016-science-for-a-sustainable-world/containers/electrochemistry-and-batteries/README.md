## What this teaches

Use the Nernst equation to connect reaction composition, electron transfer, and battery voltage.

A galvanic cell turns a spontaneous redox reaction into electrical work. The standard cell potential `E0` describes the cell under standard conditions. Real cells are not always at standard composition, so the reaction quotient `Q` changes the available voltage.
