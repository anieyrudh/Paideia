# Transfer Problem: Emergency Water Treatment

A temporary treatment unit produces clear-looking water. The final turbidity is
low, but flow is high, contact time is short, and pH is 9.1.

## Expected Reasoning

- Clear water passes only the physical clarity part of the screen.
- Short contact time lowers \(CT=C_{chlorine}t\), so microbial risk remains.
- pH 9.1 is outside the target band used in this teaching screen.
- The first operating move is to increase contact time or lower flow before
  increasing release volume.

## Rubric

- Full credit: uses turbidity, CT, pH, and flow evidence together.
- Partial credit: identifies CT but ignores pH or flow.
- No credit: approves water because it looks clear.
