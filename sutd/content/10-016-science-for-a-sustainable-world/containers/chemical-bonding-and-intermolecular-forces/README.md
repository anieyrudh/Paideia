## What this teaches

Use electronegativity difference, molecular structure, and particle type to connect bonds inside substances with attractions between particles.

Chemical bonds are attractions that hold atoms together inside a particle or lattice. Intermolecular forces are attractions between particles. The distinction matters: a water molecule has strong O-H covalent bonds inside it, but boiling water mainly separates water molecules from each other by overcoming hydrogen bonding between molecules.
