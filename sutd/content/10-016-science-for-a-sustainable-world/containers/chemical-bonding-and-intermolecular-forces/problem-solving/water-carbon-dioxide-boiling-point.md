# Water And Carbon Dioxide Boiling-Point Comparison

Water has polar O-H bonds and a bent shape, so the molecule has a net dipole. Hydrogen attached to oxygen makes hydrogen bonding possible between separate water molecules.

Carbon dioxide has polar C=O bonds, but the molecule is linear. The two bond dipoles cancel, so carbon dioxide is nonpolar overall and its main intermolecular force is London dispersion.

The same concept transfers: classify bonds, check whether shape cancels polarity, then identify the strongest attraction between particles.
