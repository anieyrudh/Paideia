# Problem-Solving Algorithm

1. Identify the photon wavelength and semiconductor band gap.
2. Convert wavelength to photon energy with \(E_{photon}=hc/\lambda\).
3. Compare \(E_{photon}\) with \(E_g\).
4. If \(E_{photon}<E_g\), mark the photon as below-gap and not productively absorbed.
5. If \(E_{photon}\ge E_g\), mark it as absorbed and compute excess energy:
   \(E_{excess}=E_{photon}-E_g\).
6. Interpret \(E_g\) as the useful threshold energy and \(E_{excess}\) as
   thermalisation in this simplified model.
