# Mindmap

- Solar energy and band theory
  - Light as photons
    - Wavelength \(\lambda\)
    - Energy \(E = hc/\lambda\)
  - Semiconductor bands
    - Valence band
    - Conduction band
    - Band gap \(E_g\)
  - Absorption rule
    - Below gap: no electron-hole pair
    - At or above gap: absorption can occur
  - Losses
    - Excess photon energy becomes heat
    - Band gap selection is a trade-off
  - Sustainable design
    - Match material to solar spectrum
    - Balance absorption and thermalisation
