# Problem-Solving Algorithm

1. Convert charge and distance values to SI units: C and m.
2. Use the source charge and separation to compute field magnitude with `E = k|Q|/r^2`.
3. Assign field direction: away from positive source charge, toward negative source charge.
4. Multiply by the test charge: `F = qE`.
5. Interpret the force direction from the sign of `q`, then state units and assumptions.
