# Coulomb's Law and Discrete Charge Fields

## Core Claim

The electric field at a point is set by the source charge and separation; the force on a test charge is the field multiplied by the test charge.

## Why It Matters

Discrete charge models let engineers reason about sensors, electrostatic actuation, charge separation, and safety limits before moving to continuous charge distributions.

## Learner Should Be Able To

- Compute field strength from `E = kQ/r^2` with SI units.
- Decide field direction from the sign of the source charge.
- Decide force direction from the sign of the test charge.
- Explain why changing the test charge changes force but not the field at the point.

## Misconception Target

Field strength is not the same as force on every charge. A small probe charge and a larger probe charge sample the same field but experience different force magnitudes, and opposite signs reverse the force direction.
