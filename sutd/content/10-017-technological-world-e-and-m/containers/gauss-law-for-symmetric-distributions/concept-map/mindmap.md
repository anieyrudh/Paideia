# Gauss Law for Symmetric Distributions

- Electric flux
  - Counts normal field through a surface
  - Depends on area and orientation
- Enclosed charge
  - Sets total closed-surface flux
  - Ignores charges outside the surface for net flux
- Gaussian surface choice
  - Sphere for centered charge or spherical shells
  - Cylinder for long line charge
  - Pillbox for infinite plane charge
- Field readout
  - Use `Phi_E = Q_enc / epsilon0`
  - Use symmetry to write `Phi_E = E A_G`
  - Interpret sign as outward or inward flux
- Transfer
  - Coaxial cable field checks
  - Capacitor and conductor field estimates
