## Misconception evidence

- "Gauss law only works for spheres" is addressed by contrasting the law's closed-surface form with symmetry-specific field extraction.
- "Flux equals field strength" is addressed by the prediction that total flux remains fixed while field changes with Gaussian area.
- "End caps always matter" is addressed by the cylindrical-symmetry surface readout.
