# Misconception Audit

## A dielectric blocks the field completely

Learners may reason from everyday insulation and treat the dielectric as a
perfect barrier. The simulation asks for a prediction before reveal, then shows
that the field remains `E = V / d` for the chosen voltage and gap while
capacitance changes through `kappa`.

## Voltage alone sets stored energy

Learners may remember `U = qV` loosely and conclude that fixed voltage means
fixed energy. The formula panel keeps `U = 1/2 CV^2` beside `Q = CV`, making the
fixed-voltage comparison explicit.

## Capacitance is supplied by the battery

The controls separate geometry, material, and voltage. Area, gap, and dielectric
constant change capacitance; voltage changes charge and energy after the
capacitance is set.
