# Sources

These sources support the ideal parallel-plate capacitor model, dielectric
polarisation framing, and fixed-voltage energy comparison used in this
container.

1. Griffiths, D. J. *Introduction to Electrodynamics*. 4th ed. Cambridge
   University Press, 2017.
   - Use: dielectric polarisation, permittivity, and capacitor energy.
   - License: publisher copyright.

2. Halliday, D., Resnick, R., and Walker, J. *Fundamentals of Physics*. 11th ed.
   Wiley, 2018.
   - Use: introductory capacitor equations and energy-storage interpretation.
   - License: publisher copyright.

3. MIT OpenCourseWare. *8.02SC Physics II: Electricity and Magnetism*,
   capacitors and dielectrics materials, 2011.
   - Use: open educational cross-check for capacitor and dielectric learning
     sequence.
   - License: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

No diagrams, textbook figures, proprietary assets, or GPL code were copied into
this container. Media is original SVG created for this PR.
