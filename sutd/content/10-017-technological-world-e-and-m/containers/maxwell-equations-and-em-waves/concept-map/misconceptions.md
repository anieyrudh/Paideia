# Misconceptions

## EM waves require conduction current everywhere

Maxwell's displacement-current term is often treated as a mathematical patch. In fact it is the term that lets changing electric fields source magnetic circulation where no charges are crossing the surface.

## Electric and magnetic waves are separate waves

The E and B fields are coupled components of one transverse electromagnetic wave. Their amplitudes are related by B0 = E0 / v.

## Higher permittivity makes the wave faster

For a simple non-dispersive medium, v = c / sqrt(epsilon_r mu_r). Increasing epsilon_r or mu_r lowers speed at fixed frequency.
