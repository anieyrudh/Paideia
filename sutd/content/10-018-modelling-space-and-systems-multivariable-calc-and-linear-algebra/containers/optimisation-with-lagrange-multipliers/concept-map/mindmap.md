# Mindmap

- Optimisation with Lagrange multipliers
  - Objective
    - \(f(x,y)\)
    - objective gradient
    - tangent derivative
  - Constraint
    - \(g(x,y)=c\)
    - feasible curve
    - constraint gradient as normal
  - Optimum condition
    - \(\nabla f=\lambda\nabla g\)
    - zero first-order tangent gain
    - lambda as sensitivity
  - Transfer
    - material budget
    - capacity constraint
    - design tradeoff
