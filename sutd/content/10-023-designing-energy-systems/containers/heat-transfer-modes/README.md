## What this teaches

Compare conduction, convection, and radiation as different pathways for heat-flow rate.

Heat transfer is energy crossing a boundary because of a temperature difference. The mode names describe the pathway: conduction through matter, convection between a surface and moving fluid, and radiation by electromagnetic emission. The same hot and cold temperatures can produce different heat-flow rates because each pathway has different coefficients and geometry.
