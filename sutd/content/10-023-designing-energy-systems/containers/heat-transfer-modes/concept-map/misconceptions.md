# Misconceptions

## Heat and temperature are the same quantity

Heat is energy transfer rate in this sim; temperature is the state variable
whose difference drives the transfer.

## Radiation needs air or contact

Radiation is not convection. It depends on emissivity and absolute temperature
through the Stefan-Boltzmann term.

## Thickness does not affect conduction

For one-dimensional steady conduction, a larger path length reduces heat-flow
rate when material, area, and temperature difference stay fixed.
