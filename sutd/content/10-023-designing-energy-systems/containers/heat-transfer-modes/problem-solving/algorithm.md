# Heat Transfer Modes Problem-Solving Algorithm

1. Identify the boundary, hot side, cold side, and transfer area.
2. Convert any Celsius temperatures to kelvin before radiation calculations.
3. Match the pathway to the formula:
   - conduction: `q = k A Delta T / L`
   - convection: `q = h A Delta T`
   - radiation: `q = epsilon sigma A (T_hot^4 - T_cold^4)`
4. Substitute values with SI units.
5. Compare heat-flow rates in watts and name the design lever that changes the
   dominant term.
