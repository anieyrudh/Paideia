### P2 follow-ups (deferred)

- Add a time-resolved cascade variant once `core/signal-pathway` exposes time-stepping or once a sim composes it with `core/dynamical-systems` for feedback. Today the kernel intentionally rejects cycles.
- Add a multi-output cascade example (one cascade feeding two transcription factors with shared upstream nodes) once the SUTD curriculum needs branching.

### High-bandwidth questions surfaced

- Should the per-node sensitivity `k` be configurable per node rather than globally? Today the same `k` propagates to every node; a future tweak could let the receptor be sharper than the kinase.
