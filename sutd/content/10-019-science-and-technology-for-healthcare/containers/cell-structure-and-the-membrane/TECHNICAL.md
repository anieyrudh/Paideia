### P1 issues

- Formula colours pair `V_m` (label) and the resting-voltage bar (blue/red), with `P_K` (blue), `P_Na` (orange), and `P_Cl` (green) tied to their channel circles and legend swatches. Substitution shows the concrete `[K+]_o`, `[K+]_i`, `[Na+]_o`, `[Na+]_i`, `[Cl-]_o`, `[Cl-]_i`, and the computed `V_m`. The dominant-ion verdict ("K+ dominant; voltage near E_K", etc.) gives the per-case interpretation that prevents the misconception "the membrane is only a passive barrier".
- Manipulation visibly changes the diagram: increasing Na+ permeability swells the orange circle and depolarises the voltage bar; raising bath [K+] shifts E_K toward zero. The Playwright manipulation test commits a depolarised configuration and asserts the "Na+ dominant" readout. No P1 outstanding.

### P2 follow-ups (deferred)

### High-bandwidth questions surfaced

- Should `core/membrane-transport` grow a `gatedGhkStep(state, conductances, dt)` once a downstream container needs an action-potential time course? The current contract intentionally keeps GHK steady-state only.
- Should the diffusion-coefficient default (`1e-9 m^2/s`) be exposed as a slider rather than a constant? Today it is a fixed small-ion approximation; a future P2 could let learners contrast small-molecule and large-protein diffusion.
