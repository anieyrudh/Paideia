## Alignment Notes

- The container follows the SUTD ESD inventory and stochastic optimisation context by treating the newsvendor as a one-period decision under demand uncertainty.
- The simulation uses a discrete demand distribution so the CDF crossing is visible without relying on normal-distribution tables.
- All example probabilities and costs are authored teaching data, not claims about a real vendor.
