title: Linear Programming Feasible Region
branch: sutd
subject: esd
---

A feasible region in two-variable linear programming is the intersection of all linear inequality constraints and non-negativity constraints.

First principles: each inequality defines a half-plane; feasible solutions must satisfy every half-plane simultaneously.

To optimize a linear objective in two variables, evaluate corner points of the feasible polygon because linear objective level sets shift uniformly.
