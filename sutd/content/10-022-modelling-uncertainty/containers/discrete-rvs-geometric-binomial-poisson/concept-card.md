# Discrete RVs: Geometric, Binomial, Poisson

## First-principles explanation

A discrete random variable assigns probabilities to countable outcomes. The
binomial model counts successes in a fixed number of independent trials, the
geometric model counts trials until the first success, and the Poisson model
counts events over a fixed exposure interval.

## Key definitions

- **PMF:** assigns `P(X=k)` to each possible value.
- **Expected value:** weighted average of outcome values.
- **Variance:** weighted spread around the expected value.
- **Tail probability:** sum of probabilities over a declared event.
