# Misconception Map

## Sample means keep the population shape

- **Evidence:** Students often copy the right-skewed population shape directly onto the sampling distribution of repeated means.
- **Surface in predict?** yes
- **Correction:** The sampling distribution is built from averages. Averages still centre on the population mean, but their spread is smaller and their shape can become more bell-like as n grows.

## Larger samples change the population

- **Evidence:** Students may say that increasing sample size makes the original population less skewed or less variable.
- **Surface in predict?** yes
- **Correction:** The population stays fixed. Increasing n narrows the distribution of sample means because the standard error is sigma divided by sqrt(n).

## Normal approximation means every observation is normal

- **Evidence:** Students may treat the theorem as a claim that individual observations become normal after enough sampling.
- **Surface in predict?** no
- **Correction:** The normal approximation describes repeated sums or means, not the raw observations in the original population.
