# Projectile Motion Mini Lab

## Learning Goal

Change launch speed and angle, then connect the shape of the path to range and
time of flight.

## Big Idea

A projectile moves sideways at constant velocity while gravity changes its
vertical velocity. The two motions happen at the same time.

## Try The Simulation

Move the speed and angle sliders. Watch the path, range, maximum height, and
flight time.

## Formula

```text
range = v^2 sin(2 theta) / g
time = 2 v sin(theta) / g
height = v^2 sin^2(theta) / (2 g)
```

## Substitution

For `v = 24 m/s`, `theta = 45 degrees`, and `g = 9.81 m/s^2`, the range is
about `58.7 m`.

## Units

Speed is measured in metres per second. Range and height are measured in
metres. Time is measured in seconds.

## Result

The longest range happens near 45 degrees when launch and landing heights are
the same.

## Legend

- Blue path: projectile trajectory.
- Green marker: highest point.
- Orange marker: landing point.

## Common Misunderstanding

Students often think the projectile keeps a forward force after launch. This
model separates horizontal motion from vertical acceleration due to gravity.
