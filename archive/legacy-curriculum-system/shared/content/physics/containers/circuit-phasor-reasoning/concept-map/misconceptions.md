# Circuit Phasor Reasoning Misconception Map

## Phasors are physical rotating wires

**Why it appears:** Learners see a rotating arrow and may treat it as a literal circuit object instead of a bookkeeping vector for sinusoidal magnitude and phase.

**Evidence:** OpenStax College Physics 2e, AC circuits introduction and phasor treatment.

**Surface in predict?** no

**Correction target:** Ask what the arrow encodes: rms size and phase angle relative to the source voltage.

## Resistance makes every AC circuit in phase

**Why it appears:** Students correctly remember that a pure resistor has current in phase with voltage, then overextend that rule to mixed RLC circuits.

**Evidence:** OpenStax College Physics 2e, AC circuits and inductive/capacitive reactance sections.

**Surface in predict?** yes

**Correction target:** Compare `R` with the signed net reactance `X_L - X_C` before deciding lead or lag.

## Current is used up around a circuit

**Why it appears:** Component-by-component voltage changes can make a series circuit look like current is consumed at each part.

**Evidence:** OpenStax College Physics 2e, circuits and DC instruments chapter.

**Surface in predict?** yes

**Correction target:** State that a series AC circuit has one current through every element while component voltages can differ in phase.
