# Misconceptions

## The optimum must sit at the visual centre

The feasible region shows what is allowed, not what is best. A linear objective
adds a direction of preference. Sweeping an objective line across the polygon
usually selects a boundary point, not a visually central point.

## One satisfied inequality proves feasibility

Feasibility is simultaneous. A candidate point must satisfy every constraint,
including non-negativity and any viewport/domain limits declared for the model.

## Infeasible means no equation exists

Infeasible means no point satisfies the full system, or that the tested point is
outside the system. The individual boundary equations can still exist and be
plotted.
