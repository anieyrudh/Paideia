# Misconception Audit

## P-value as probability the null is true

The predict prompt asks for a decision when the observed mean is above the null but not far enough to reject. This surfaces whether the learner treats evidence strength as a probability of the null itself.

## Statistical significance as practical importance

The Explain stage asks for a sentence separating the decision from the real-world size of the effect. A learner who writes "reject, so the difference matters" is missing the second layer of interpretation.

## Direction alone decides rejection

Controls let the learner raise or lower the observed mean while watching the z statistic and critical boundary. This makes it visible that a sample mean in the alternative direction can still fall outside the rejection region.
