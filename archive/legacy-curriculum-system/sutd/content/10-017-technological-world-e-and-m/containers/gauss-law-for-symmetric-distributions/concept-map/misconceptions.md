# Misconceptions

## Gauss law only works for spheres

The law applies to every closed surface. Spheres are useful because spherical
symmetry makes the field normal and constant on the surface.

## Flux is field strength

Flux combines field magnitude, surface area, and the angle between the field and
surface normal. The same flux can correspond to a weaker field on a larger
matched surface.

## End caps always matter

For a long charged line, the field is radial. The cylinder end caps have zero
normal flux because the field is tangent to those faces.
