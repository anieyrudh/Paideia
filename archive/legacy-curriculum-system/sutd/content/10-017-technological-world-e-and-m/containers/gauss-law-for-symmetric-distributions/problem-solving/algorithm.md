# Problem-Solving Algorithm

1. Identify the charge symmetry: spherical, cylindrical, or planar.
2. Choose a closed Gaussian surface that makes the field normal and constant on the useful area.
3. Compute the enclosed charge `Q_enc` from charge, line charge density, or surface charge density.
4. Compute the effective Gaussian area `A_G`.
5. Apply `Phi_E = Q_enc / epsilon0` and `E = Q_enc / (epsilon0 A_G)`.
6. Check sign, units, and interpretation: outward flux for positive enclosed charge, inward flux for negative enclosed charge.
