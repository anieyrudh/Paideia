# Mindmap

- Coulomb's law and discrete charge fields
  - Source charge sets field direction
    - positive source: field points away
    - negative source: field points toward
  - Separation controls magnitude
    - inverse-square scaling
    - SI units are required
  - Test charge sets force
    - positive test charge follows the field
    - negative test charge reverses the force
  - Prepares for Gauss law
    - many discrete charges motivate symmetry arguments
