# Misconception Audit

## Resonance means zero impedance

The prediction prompt asks what remains at resonance. The reveal keeps
resistance visible in the formula and readout, so learners see that `Z = R`
when reactance cancels.

## Bigger reactance means bigger current

The controls let learners raise and lower frequency. Current peaks when
`X_L - X_C` is close to zero, not when either reactance is large.

## High Q is always better

The formula panel shows bandwidth. High Q improves selectivity, but a narrower
band can miss a detuned signal.
