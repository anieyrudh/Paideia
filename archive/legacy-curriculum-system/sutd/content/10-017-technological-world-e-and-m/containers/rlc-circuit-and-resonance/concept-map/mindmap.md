# RLC Circuit and Resonance

- AC impedance
  - `R`
  - `X_L = 2 pi f L`
  - `X_C = 1 / (2 pi f C)`
- Resonance
  - `X_L = X_C`
  - `f0 = 1 / (2 pi sqrt(LC))`
  - current maximum set by `R`
- Detuning
  - below resonance: capacitive, current leads
  - above resonance: inductive, current lags
- Design
  - tune `L` and `C`
  - reduce `R` for higher Q
  - bandwidth narrows as Q increases
