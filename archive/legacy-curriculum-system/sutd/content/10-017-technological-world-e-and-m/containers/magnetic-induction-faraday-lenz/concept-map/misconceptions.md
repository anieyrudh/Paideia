# Misconception Audit

## Lenz's law opposes the magnetic field itself

The prediction prompt asks about increasing outward flux. The revealed model
keeps the target on the change: increasing outward flux induces an inward
magnetic effect, while decreasing outward flux would induce an outward effect.

## No motion means no induced emf

The controls allow field strength to change while the loop area stays fixed.
This surfaces induction from `Delta Phi / Delta t`, not just from moving a wire.

## More turns reverse the current direction

The formula panel separates magnitude and direction. More turns multiply emf
magnitude, but Lenz direction comes from the sign of the flux change.
