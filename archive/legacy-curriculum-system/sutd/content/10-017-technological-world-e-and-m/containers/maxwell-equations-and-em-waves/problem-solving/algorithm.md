# Maxwell EM Wave Problem Algorithm

1. Identify the frequency and convert it to hertz.
2. Identify the medium's relative permittivity and relative permeability.
3. Compute the wave speed: v = c / sqrt(epsilon_r mu_r).
4. Compute wavelength: lambda = v / f.
5. If electric-field amplitude is given, compute B0 = E0 / v.
6. Use the frequency to identify the spectrum band.
7. Interpret the result physically: E and B are coupled transverse fields, not separate waves.
8. For a medium-comparison problem, keep frequency fixed across the boundary and compare wavelengths or speeds.
