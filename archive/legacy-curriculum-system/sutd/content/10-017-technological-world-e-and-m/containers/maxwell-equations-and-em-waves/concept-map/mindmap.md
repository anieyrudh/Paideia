# Mindmap

- Maxwell equations and EM waves
  - Prerequisites
    - Electric fields
    - Magnetic fields
    - Faraday induction
  - Core claims
    - Changing E and B fields sustain each other
    - EM waves are transverse
    - Speed depends on permittivity and permeability
  - Quantities
    - Frequency
    - Wavelength
    - Wave speed
    - E-field and B-field amplitudes
  - Misconceptions
    - Conduction current is required everywhere
    - E and B are separate waves
    - Larger permittivity makes waves faster
