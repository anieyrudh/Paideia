# Misconception Map

## Inducer immediately increases protein

Evidence: Alon, *An Introduction to Systems Biology*, gene-expression response and degradation-rate examples.

Surface in predict? Yes.

The prediction asks about steady-state mRNA and protein together. The wrong "protein rises immediately while mRNA stays at baseline" choice surfaces the idea that inducer acts directly on protein abundance, skipping the mRNA intermediate and the finite production/decay path.

## Hill responses are linear

Evidence: Hill's dose-response form and standard systems-biology treatments of saturating promoter activation.

Surface in predict? Yes.

The prediction contrasts a saturating plateau with unlimited linear growth. The sim then places the operating point on the Hill curve so learners can see the steep threshold region and the plateau above several `K`.

## Coding strand and template strand are interchangeable

Evidence: OpenStax Biology 2e, gene-expression chapter, transcription strand convention.

Surface in predict? No.

The concept card and algorithm explicitly state that this container's presets are coding-strand DNA. A future free-input mode should ask learners which strand they supplied before transcription.
