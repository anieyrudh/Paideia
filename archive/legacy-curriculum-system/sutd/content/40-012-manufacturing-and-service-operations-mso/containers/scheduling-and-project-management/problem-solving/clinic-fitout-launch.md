# Clinic Fit-Out Launch

A clinic fit-out has design, procurement, installation, staff training, and
licensing activities. Design takes 4 days. Procurement takes 5 days after
design. Installation takes 7 days after procurement. Staff training takes 6 days
after design. Licensing takes 3 days after both installation and training.

Compute the project duration, identify the critical path, and explain which
activity can slip without moving launch.
