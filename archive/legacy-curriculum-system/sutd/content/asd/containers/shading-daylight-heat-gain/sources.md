- URL: https://www.wbdg.org/resources/sun-control-and-shading-devices

- Relevance: Establishes why external shading is used to control solar gains and glare before detailed mechanical sizing.

- Year: 2014
- Relevance: Supports the daylight design framing that larger windows and shading devices must be evaluated as a combined visual-comfort strategy.
- Container use: Supports the misconception correction that more glass is not automatically better daylight quality.
