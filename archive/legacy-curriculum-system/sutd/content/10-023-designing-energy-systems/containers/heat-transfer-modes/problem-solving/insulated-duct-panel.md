# Transfer Rubric: Insulated Duct Panel

A strong answer:

- separates conduction through the insulation, convection from the surface to
  room air, and radiation from the warm surface;
- uses kelvin for the radiation temperatures and a kelvin difference for the
  conduction/convection terms;
- explains that thicker insulation changes `L`, airflow changes `h`, and
  low-emissivity coating changes `epsilon`;
- reports heat-flow rate in watts, not degrees Celsius.
