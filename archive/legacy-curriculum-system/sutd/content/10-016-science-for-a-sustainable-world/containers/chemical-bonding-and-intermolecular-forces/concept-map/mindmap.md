# Mindmap

- Chemical bonding
  - Electronegativity difference
    - large difference: mostly ionic
    - moderate difference: polar covalent
    - small difference: nonpolar covalent
  - Molecular structure
    - shape can cancel bond dipoles
    - particle type affects attraction
  - Intermolecular forces
    - ion-ion attractions
    - hydrogen bonding
    - dipole-dipole forces
    - London dispersion forces
  - Applications
    - materials
    - water treatment
    - batteries
