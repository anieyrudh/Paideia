# Algorithm

1. Identify the atoms or particle type.
2. Estimate the electronegativity difference for the bonded atoms.
3. Classify the bond as mostly ionic, polar covalent, or nonpolar covalent.
4. Decide whether molecular shape cancels or preserves bond dipoles.
5. Identify the strongest attraction between particles: ion-ion attraction, hydrogen bonding, dipole-dipole forces, or London dispersion forces.
6. Use that attraction to reason about melting point, boiling point, solubility, or material behaviour.
