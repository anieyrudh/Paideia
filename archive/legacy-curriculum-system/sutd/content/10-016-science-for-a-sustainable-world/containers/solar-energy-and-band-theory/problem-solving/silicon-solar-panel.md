# Transfer Problem: Silicon Solar Panel

A team is choosing how to explain sunlight absorption for a silicon panel. Use
\(E_g=1.1\ \mathrm{eV}\) and compare three photons:

- blue: \(450\ \mathrm{nm}\)
- red: \(700\ \mathrm{nm}\)
- near-infrared: \(1100\ \mathrm{nm}\)

## Expected Reasoning

- Compute photon energy with \(E_{photon}=hc/\lambda\).
- Blue photons are well above the gap and lose more excess energy as heat.
- Red photons are still above the gap for silicon and can be absorbed.
- Near-infrared photons at the edge must be checked numerically against the
  1.1 eV threshold.

## Rubric

- Full credit: uses the formula, includes units, compares each photon to the
  band gap, and explains both absorption and thermalisation.
- Partial credit: compares wavelengths qualitatively but omits the threshold
  calculation.
- No credit: says higher irradiance alone makes below-gap photons useful.
