## What this teaches

Use photon energy and semiconductor band gaps to explain why solar cell materials absorb some wavelengths and lose excess energy as heat.

The electromagnetic wave arrives as photons with energy set by wavelength. The semiconductor provides discrete allowed bands separated by a gap. A photon can create an electron-hole pair only when its energy is at least the gap: the threshold is a comparison of two energies, not a statement about brightness alone. Above threshold, the band gap sets the useful excitation energy in this simple model, while extra photon energy is quickly dissipated to the lattice.
