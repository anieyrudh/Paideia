# Transfer Problem: Coastal Land-Use Plan

A coastal region proposes 2.5% annual habitat conversion and 1.0% annual restoration.
The starting intact habitat is 75% and species sensitivity is 1.5.

## Expected Reasoning

- Net conversion pressure is still positive.
- Habitat should be projected across multiple years, not judged from one year.
- Biodiversity response is nonlinear through \(B=100(H_t/H_0)^z\).
- The plan should be revised if the final biodiversity index enters the moderate
  or high-risk band.

## Rubric

- Full credit: compares rates, projects habitat, substitutes into the formula,
  and interprets risk.
- Partial credit: compares rates but omits the biodiversity index.
- No credit: says the effect is small because 2.5% sounds small.
