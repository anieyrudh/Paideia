# Mindmap

- Divergence
  - Local expansion or contraction
  - Positive source, negative sink
  - Uses `dP/dx + dQ/dy`
- Curl
  - Local signed spin
  - Positive counter-clockwise in 2D
  - Uses `dQ/dx - dP/dy`
- Modelling choice
  - Source/sink behaviour
  - Circulation behaviour
  - Conservative-field checks
