# Misconception Map

## Equilibrium means nothing changes anywhere

Evidence: Strogatz, *Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos*, phase-plane chapters.

Surface in predict? Yes.

The equilibrium point itself has zero derivative, but the surrounding vector
field still tells nearby states how to move. The prediction asks what a nearby
trajectory does after it is released, so selecting "Cross the equilibrium and
stop there" surfaces this misconception.

## Arrows show only physical velocity

Evidence: Hirsch, Smale, and Devaney, *Differential Equations, Dynamical
Systems, and an Introduction to Chaos*.

Surface in predict? Yes.

Phase-plane arrows show rates of change for abstract state variables, not
necessarily physical motion through space. The vector-field plot labels the
arrows as state-change directions and pairs them with trace-determinant
classification.
