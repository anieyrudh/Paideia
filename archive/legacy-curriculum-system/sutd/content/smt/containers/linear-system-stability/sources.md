# Sources

These sources support the trace-determinant test, eigenvalue stability
classification, and linearisation framing used in this container.

1. Strogatz, S. H. *Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos*. 2nd ed. Westview Press, 2015.
   - Use: stability of fixed points, trace-determinant plane, spirals, nodes, saddles.
   - License: publisher copyright.

2. Hirsch, M. W., Smale, S., and Devaney, R. L. *Differential Equations,
   Dynamical Systems, and an Introduction to Chaos*. 3rd ed. Academic Press,
   2013.
   - Use: linear systems, eigendirections, qualitative phase-plane behaviour.
   - License: publisher copyright.

3. Boyce, W. E., DiPrima, R. C., and Meade, D. B. *Elementary Differential
   Equations and Boundary Value Problems*. 11th ed. Wiley, 2017.
   - Use: worked conventions for two-dimensional linear systems and eigenvalue cases.
   - License: publisher copyright.
