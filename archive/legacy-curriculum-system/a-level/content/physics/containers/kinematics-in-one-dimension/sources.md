## Alignment notes

- The syllabus source anchors the A-Level requirement to distinguish distance,
  displacement, speed, velocity, and acceleration, and to use constant
  acceleration equations.
- The OpenStax source supports the conceptual treatment of one-dimensional
  motion, constant acceleration, and sign conventions.
