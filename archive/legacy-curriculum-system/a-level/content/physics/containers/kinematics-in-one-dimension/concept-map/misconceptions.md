# Misconceptions

## Velocity and acceleration must have the same sign

**Evidence**: OpenStax University Physics Volume 1, Section 3.4 Motion with Constant Acceleration.

**Surface in predict?** no

**Description**: Students may think a negative acceleration means motion in the negative direction, rather than a velocity that is changing in the negative direction.

## Average velocity equals final velocity

**Evidence**: OpenStax University Physics Volume 1, Section 3.4 Motion with Constant Acceleration.

**Surface in predict?** yes

**Description**: Students may multiply final velocity by time for accelerated motion, missing that displacement comes from the whole velocity-time area.

## Distance and displacement are interchangeable

**Evidence**: Singapore-Cambridge H2 A Level 9478 Physics syllabus for examination in 2027, kinematics learning outcomes.

**Surface in predict?** no

**Description**: Students may treat path length and signed change in position as the same quantity, especially when motion reverses direction.
