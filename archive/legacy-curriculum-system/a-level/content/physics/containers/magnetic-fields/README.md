## What this teaches

Use Fleming's left-hand rule and magnetic-force formulae to connect field direction, current, moving charges, and circular paths.

A magnetic field describes a region where moving charge can experience a force. The force is not along the magnetic field line. For a current-carrying wire or a moving positive charge, the force is perpendicular to both the direction of motion or conventional current and the magnetic field direction.
