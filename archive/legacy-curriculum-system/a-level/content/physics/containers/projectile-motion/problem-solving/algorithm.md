# Projectile Motion Problem-Solving Algorithm

1. Sketch the launch point, landing level, and positive axes.
2. Resolve the initial velocity into horizontal and vertical components.
3. Write the vertical equation that reaches the landing height.
4. Solve the vertical equation for the positive time of flight.
5. Use the horizontal component and time of flight to find range.
6. Check units and signs: horizontal range is a distance; vertical velocity may
   be positive on the way up and negative on the way down.
7. Interpret whether the answer depends on launch speed, angle, height, or
   gravity.

Keep the horizontal and vertical equations separate until the shared time
connects them.
