# Electric Fields Problem-Solving Algorithm

## 1. Identify the source and the test charge

Mark the source charge `Q` as the object creating the field. Mark the test charge `q` as the object feeling force or changing energy.

## 2. Decide whether the question asks for field, force, or energy

- Field: use the source and position only.
- Force: calculate the field first, then multiply by the test charge.
- Energy: use the potential difference and the moving charge.

## 3. Find the field direction

For a point charge, draw the radial line from the source to the point.

- Positive source: field points away from the source.
- Negative source: field points towards the source.

## 4. Calculate the field strength

Use:

```text
E = kQ / r^2
```

Substitute `Q` in coulombs and `r` in metres. The magnitude uses `|Q|`; the sign is handled by the direction arrow.

## 5. Calculate the force on the test charge

Use:

```text
F = qE
```

If `q` is positive, the force points with the field. If `q` is negative, the force points opposite the field.

## 6. Connect potential difference to energy

Use:

```text
Delta U = q Delta V
```

Keep the sign of `q`. A negative result means electric potential energy decreases for that move.

## 7. Interpret the answer

State the physical meaning in one sentence: what sets the field, what sets the force direction, and whether the charge gains or loses electric potential energy.
