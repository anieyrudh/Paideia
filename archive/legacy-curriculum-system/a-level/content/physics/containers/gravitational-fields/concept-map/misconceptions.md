# Misconception Coverage

## Field strength is constant near every mass

Evidence: Singapore-Cambridge H2 Physics 9478 syllabus distinguishes field strength from the near-Earth approximation and requires inverse-square gravitational fields.

Surface in predict? Yes. The prediction asks what happens when radius doubles.

The prediction asks what happens when radius doubles. The sim shows both the inward arrow and the numeric inverse-square comparison so learners separate direction from magnitude.

## Potential energy must be positive

Evidence: OpenStax University Physics Volume 1, Section 13.4 defines gravitational potential energy with zero at infinity for attractive fields.

Surface in predict? No. This is surfaced after reveal in the potential and potential-energy readouts.

The revealed formula block uses zero at infinity and shows `phi = -GM / r` alongside `E_p = m phi`, with units and substituted values.

## A heavier probe changes the field strength at a point

Evidence: Singapore-Cambridge H2 Physics 9478 syllabus defines gravitational field strength as force per unit mass, separating field strength from force on a selected test mass.

Surface in predict? No. This is surfaced by the probe-mass manipulation and readout comparison.

The probe-mass control changes force and potential energy readouts but leaves field strength fixed when source mass and radius are unchanged.
