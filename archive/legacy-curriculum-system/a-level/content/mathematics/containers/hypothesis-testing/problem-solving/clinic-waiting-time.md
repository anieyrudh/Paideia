# Transfer Problem: Clinic Waiting Time

**Prompt:** A clinic claims its mean waiting time is 18 minutes. A sample of 49 visits has mean 16.1 minutes. The population standard deviation is known to be 5.6 minutes. At the 5% significance level, decide whether there is evidence that the mean waiting time is less than 18 minutes.

## Worked Route

1. \(H_0: \mu = 18\), \(H_1: \mu < 18\).
2. \(SE = 5.6 / \sqrt{49} = 0.8\) minutes.
3. \(z = (16.1 - 18) / 0.8 = -2.375\).
4. For a 5% lower-tailed test, the critical region is \(z \le -1.645\).
5. Since \(-2.375\) is in the critical region, reject \(H_0\).
6. Context: there is evidence at the 5% level that the clinic's mean waiting time is less than 18 minutes.
