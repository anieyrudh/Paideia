# Probability And Statistics Problem-Solving Algorithm

1. **Define the random variable.** State what X measures and list the possible values with units where relevant.
2. **Build the distribution.** Convert counts or weights into probabilities and check that the total probability is 1.
3. **Find the centre and spread.** Use E(X) for the long-run mean and Var(X) for the distribution spread.
4. **State the null model.** Identify the mean or parameter assumed by H0 before looking at the sample decision.
5. **Standardise the sample evidence.** Compare the observed sample mean with the null mean using the standard error.
6. **Make the decision in context.** Compare the test statistic with the rejection region and write what that means for the situation.
